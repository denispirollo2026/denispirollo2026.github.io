Hawkesbury · what I'll do

Five points.

Denis declared these five points himself, in public. They lead everything the campaign does. The full plans are posted below.

Point 1

Fix the worst roads first.

Fix our high-priority roads and infrastructure. We fix the worst ones first, and we do them properly, so we do not have to do them again.
Point 2

Revitalize Main Street, waterfront, festivals.

Main Street, the waterfront and the festivals are one job, not three. We want a downtown worth walking in, a river we actually use, and reasons to get together again.
Point 3

Keep our young people and jobs.

Keep our young people here, bring more in, and bring the jobs back. Young people stay when there is good work here.
Point 4

Build somewhere to live.

Bring in investors to build here. People need somewhere to live and we have almost nothing left to rent or buy.
Point 5

Open the books.

Every candidate can yap about what we want to do. Me included. But nothing gets done until an open audit of the books shows where our money is going. It is last on this list because it is the one that makes the other four possible.
